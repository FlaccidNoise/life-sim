life-sim — what you're seeing
PHOTO — plant; light → energy (base food)
DIGESTIVE — passive eater; eats plants/meat on contact
MEAT — corpse; decaying food
MOTILE — smells food, swims up-gradient (reflex)
SENSOR — critter's gradient sensor (M4)
NEURON — critter's brain (recurrent net)
MOVER — critter body; driven by its neurons
PREDATOR — carnivore mouth; hunts & latches onto foragers
BOND (O) — link between cells; brighter = more energy flowing
FORAGER — the multicellular brain-organism: a MOVER body + 2 SENSORs + 2 NEURONs + a leading DIGESTIVE mouth, developed from a genome. It smells the plant gradient, swims to food, eats, and reproduces when fed (offspring = mutated clone).
PREDATOR (red) — a brain-organism that hunts foragers: it ambush-loiters in their scent, latches on and drains them, while foragers FLEE the predator-scent gradient. Three trophic levels — plants → foragers → predators — coexist and breathe: bloom → strip → starve (→ MEAT) → regrow → repeat. Press X to snap the camera onto the nearest hunter.
Edible cells emit a diffusing smell; movers & foragers climb its gradient (chemotaxis). Cells repel on contact. Energy ≤ 0 → die → meat → decay; energy ≥ threshold → divide.
Brain (B): nodes = neurons (blue −, grey 0, orange +); edges = synapses (green +, red −, width = |weight|).
