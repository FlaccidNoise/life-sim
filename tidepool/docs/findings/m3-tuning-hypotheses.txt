== Part 5, item 2: the egg range (2026-10-14) ==
Change, on a scratch tree only: EGG_RANGE in crates/sim/src/genome.rs from (0.2, 0.7) to
(0.2, 0.9). The founder's egg stays 0.7 (birth_growth). Every other value as Part 4 leaves it,
with mix_rate=1 as a flag. Read: probe-census on seeds 1, 4, 5, 6, 8 to tick 144,000, every
36,000, for egg and len, against the same census at the range as it is.
Hypothesis: the egg sits against the top of its range (0.64 to 0.68 against 0.7 in M2) because
selection pushes it up: a larger newborn survives better than more small ones. If so, with room
above, the mean egg at tick 144,000 rises above 0.7, to 0.72 or more on at least 4 of 5 seeds.
len is the gene of the adult and the egg sets only the newborn's growth, so len stays in its band
(7.8 to 22.2) on 5 of 5 and within 1.5 of the reading at the range as it is.
If the egg stays at 0.70 or below on most seeds, the top of the range was not what held it there,
and the range as it is (or the egg as a parameter) is the proposal.
Confound: the step of a life mutation is mut_sigma * (max - min), so the egg's step grows from
0.040 to 0.056. A rise could be faster drift as well as selection.
