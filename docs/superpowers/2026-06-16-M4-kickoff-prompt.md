## Key Discoveries / Conventions (a fresh session WILL get these wrong otherwise)
- **`core/cells` and `core/environment` take SCALAR params, never `Config`; `core/physics` takes raw typed arrays, never `CellArrays`.** `World.tick` unpacks `cfg.*` at the call site. - **Determinism is engineered** (byte-identical `(Config, seed)` incl. all SoA + neuron state): canonical ascending iteration order; per-subsystem seeded substreams via `rng.split(streamId)` — **light uses `split(1)`; the M3 reflex draws NO RNG; give any M4 neural init/noise a distinct streamId (e.g. `split(2)`)**. The reproducibility test (`core/sim/reproducibility.test.ts`) seeds motiles and asserts byte-identical `heading` — extend it to assert byte-identical neuron activations.
- **Double-buffering (the M4 crux):** THINK reads activations at t−1 and writes t (matches the §4.6 equation + the M4 unit test). **Open decisions to settle with Josh before finalizing the plan (flag them — these are genuine M4 design forks):**
1. **Critter cohesion without bonds.** The acceptance is a "multicellular critter [that] swims to food," but bonds/springs are M5 and the spec says M4 "needs no bond dynamics." So how do the sensor + neuron + mover cells stay together to swim coherently? Options: (a) co-located cells sharing `organismId`, no springs, brain drives the mover, accept some drift over the short demo horizon; (b) an M4-only minimal rigid grouping (cells move as a fixed offset from the mover); (c) validate the brain numerically (golden THINK test) and let the *visible* "swims to food" be a single mover cell whose thrust the hand-wired brain computes from a co-located sensor. Recommend settling toward (a)/(c) — keep physical cohesion entirely in M5; M4 proves the *brain* works.
2. **Sensor/mover cell split — does it start here?** In M3 (its decision 1) Josh wanted *separate* sensor and mover cells for future evolving morphology (sensors front, movers back), but M3 built a combined MOTILE because there was no wiring substrate. **M4 is the wiring substrate.** So M4 is the natural place to introduce a `SENSOR` cell (senses, doesn't move) + `NEURON` + `MOVER` cell, wired by synapses — realizing that vision. Alternative: keep MOTILE as combined sensor+mover and just insert NEURON between its sense and act. Recommend the split (SENSOR + NEURON + MOVER) since it's the whole point of having a brain — but confirm.
3. **How neuron outputs drive the mover, and the sensor→neuron binding.** A designated mover-input neuron's activation → thrust magnitude (and a second → turn)? Sensor channels (`senseX`/`senseY`, plus the §4.6 vocabulary: own energy, ambient light, local density, an internal clock/oscillator) → which become input neurons in M4? Recommend a minimal M4 input set (the food gradient + an internal clock for oscillation) and 1–2 output neurons (thrust, turn), expandable later.
4. **`neuralCost` in M4 or deferred.** §4.5 lists a per-active-neuron/synapse cost. Include a minimal `neuralCost` knob now (so a brain isn't free) or defer the economy coupling to keep M4 focused on the mechanism? Recommend a knob present-but-small.
